The preview displays CR, LF and CRLF as line breaks. The original clipboard text is shown below as a quoted string: \r means carriage return, \n means line feed, \t means tab and \\ means a literal backslash. Unicode format characters, including bidirectional controls and zero-width joiners, appear as \uXXXX escapes (two escapes for a supplementary character); this does not remove them from the paste. Other characters remain literal, so this is not a complete code-point listing. On delivery, each CRLF pair, lone CR or LF becomes one carriage return.
